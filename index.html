01Our Approach

01 — Comparative Prompt Audit

Compare prompts with and without queer identity terms. Ensure consistency across platforms, styles, aspect ratios and output quantities. Record recurring visual patterns and rejected prompts.

Ungless, Ross & Lauscher (2023) — Stereotypes and Smut: The (Mis)representation of Non-cisgender Identities by Text-to-Image Models

Treat the prototype as part of the research process. Test, modify and optimise the website through repeated iteration.
